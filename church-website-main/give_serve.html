Ways To Give

Faithfulness in giving offerings and paying tithes to enhance the ministry of the church is emphasized.

"Each of you should give what you have decided in your heart to give, not reluctantly or under compulsion, for God loves a cheerful giver."

2 Corinthians 9:7

Electronically

  1. Go to M-Pesa on your phone
  2. Select Lipa na M-Pesa
  3. Select Paybill
  4. Enter Paybill Number: 247247
  5. Enter Account Number: 224038
  6. Enter Amount
  7. Enter PIN and confirm

Cheque

You can as well give by writing a cheque payable to JTLIM Church.

In Person

Access to a POS terminal and/or an offering envelope provide simple ways to give to the church during service.
